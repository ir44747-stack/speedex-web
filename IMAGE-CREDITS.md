## Rights caution before commercial launch

Wikimedia’s page for “Doha Skyline 10.jpg” carries a Qatar freedom-of-panorama warning: Qatar does not provide a general freedom-of-panorama exception for copyrighted architectural/artistic works, so a Creative Commons licence from the photographer may not by itself clear every underlying architectural right for every commercial use. This caution should be reviewed for all Doha skyline photography before paid advertising or other prominent commercial exploitation in Qatar. This file records provenance and the photographer’s licence; it is not legal advice.
